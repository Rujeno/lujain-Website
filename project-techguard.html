The project in one view

This is a portfolio prototype, but it is designed around a real operating question: can a technology team see ownership, exposure, evidence, and next action in one place?

The challenge

IT governance work is often spread across spreadsheets, email threads, access lists, risk logs, evidence folders, and ticketing tools. The information exists, but the relationship between the records is difficult to see.

The response

I designed a single operating view that connects the records through ownership and action: an asset can have an owner, an access review can reveal a gap, a risk can require treatment, and a control can need evidence or a ticket.

The operating model

The experience follows the way governance work actually moves: establish what exists, confirm who can access it, assess exposure, prove the control, and act on the gap.

What I contributed

The project demonstrates both the technology work and the reasoning behind the experience.

01 · PRODUCT THINKING

GRC translated into workflow

I converted abstract governance concepts into records, owners, statuses, due dates, evidence, and next actions.

02 · INTERFACE DESIGN

A decision-oriented dashboard

The visual hierarchy surfaces control health, high risks, reviews due, and open tickets before the detail.

03 · FRONTEND BUILD

A working, inspectable MVP

I built the interactive front end with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, including navigation, forms, filters, and synthetic records.

Scope note: TechGuard is a portfolio MVP using synthetic data. It is not connected to a live organization, real identities, or production systems.
