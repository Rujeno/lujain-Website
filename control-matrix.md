# TechGuard Control Matrix

This matrix is a demonstration artifact for the portfolio project. It is not a compliance certification or a substitute for an organization-specific control assessment.

| ID | Control objective | Evidence example | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AC-02 | Review user and privileged access periodically | Signed access review record | IT / business owner | Due |
| AC-04 | Revoke access when a user leaves or changes role | Revocation log and offboarding checklist | HR / IT | In review |
| AS-03 | Maintain accountability for technology assets | Asset register and custody acknowledgement | IT | Complete |
| OP-01 | Define and monitor technology services | IT service catalog and SLA report | IT | Complete |
| IR-01 | Record incidents and learn from recurring issues | Incident register and trend report | IT | Complete |
| TP-02 | Track third-party service obligations | Vendor SLA and review evidence | Procurement / IT | Gap |
| DG-01 | Assign ownership and handling expectations to important data | Data inventory and owner register | Business owner | In progress |

## Evidence quality checklist

1. The evidence is dated.
2. The owner is identifiable.
3. The scope is clear.
4. The approval or review decision is recorded.
5. The evidence is linked to a specific control objective.
6. Exceptions have an owner and target date.
